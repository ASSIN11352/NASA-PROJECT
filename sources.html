PROJECT NOTES

What to verify before submission

01

Dates and mission status

Confirm launch, landing, last contact, and official end-of-mission dates with NASA mission pages.

02

Scientific claims

Describe evidence carefully. Evidence of ancient water does not mean life was found.

03

Image attribution

Record the original image page, credit line, and any usage restrictions. Do not imply NASA endorses the project.
